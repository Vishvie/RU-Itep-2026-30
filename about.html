About Our Community

A legacy of learning, a future of possibilities.

Our Story

The ITEP programme at Ravenshaw University, Cuttack, is one of the most prestigious teacher education courses in Odisha. Our batch of 2026-2030 brings together a diverse group of aspiring educators, each with unique strengths and perspectives, united by a shared passion for shaping the future of education.

Our Mission

We believe that teachers are the architects of society. Our mission is to develop competent, compassionate, and innovative educators who can inspire the next generation. Through rigorous academic training and practical experience, we are preparing ourselves to make a lasting impact in classrooms across the country.

Ravenshaw University

Established in 1868, Ravenshaw University is one of the oldest and most distinguished universities in Eastern India. Located in the historic city of Cuttack, it has been a beacon of learning for over 150 years, producing some of Odisha's most celebrated leaders, thinkers, and educators.

This Website

This community website was built to preserve and celebrate our journey together. It serves as a digital yearbook, event archive, and connection hub for all batchmates and faculty. Here you can browse student and teacher profiles, revisit event memories, discover the talents within our batch, and stay connected long after we graduate.
